Ask a Pawfessional

Questions, answered.

What does my pup need before their first ride?

A Pup Passport (profile), a signed liability waiver, and current Rabies, DHPP and Bordetella vaccines. Pups should be at least 6 months old, spayed or neutered after 7 months, and comfortable around other dogs. Every new rider does a free 30-minute meet-and-greet first.

How do you keep dogs safe on the bus?

Every seat has a harness clip-in, the bus is climate controlled, and we never leave pups unattended. Our crew is pet CPR and first-aid certified, and we carry water, cooling towels and a full first-aid kit.

What about Arizona heat?

From May through September we start earlier, choose water and shaded stops, and check pavement temperature before every walk. If it's too hot for paws, the route changes to indoor and water-based fun.

How does the live tracker work?

The bus shares its location during trips and our crew posts to Instagram at each stop. Those Pupdates appear right on the map, so you can follow the whole day.

How are merit badges earned?

Each stop has its own badge, earned on a pup's first visit. There are also milestone badges for 1, 5 and 10 rides, and grade badges for top marks on the report card.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel by 6 PM the day before for a full credit. Same-day cancellations are charged half the ride price because your pup's seat was held.
