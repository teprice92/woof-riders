The humans
Liability waiver & release

1. Assumption of risk

I understand that field trips involve transportation, outdoor activity, off-leash and on-leash play with other dogs, water, heat, uneven terrain and wildlife. These carry inherent risks including bites, scratches, illness, parasites, heat stress, injury and, rarely, death. I voluntarily accept these risks on behalf of my dog.

2. Health and vaccinations

I confirm my dog is in good health, current on Rabies, DHPP and Bordetella vaccines, and on flea and tick prevention. I will tell Woof Riders about any change in health or behavior before my dog's next ride.

3. Transportation

I authorize Woof Riders to transport my dog in its vehicles to and from field trip destinations, secured with a harness restraint.

4. Behavior and dismissal

I confirm my dog has no history of aggression except as disclosed. Woof Riders may separate, send home or decline future service for any dog whose behavior puts people or other dogs at risk. I am responsible for any damage or injury my dog causes.

5. Emergency veterinary care

If my dog needs urgent care and I can't be reached, I authorize Woof Riders to take my dog to the nearest available veterinarian and approve treatment up to $1,000. I agree to pay all resulting veterinary costs.

6. Photos and social media

I allow Woof Riders to photograph and film my dog and share those images on its website, Instagram and other channels, including on the live trip tracker.

7. Release and indemnity

To the fullest extent allowed by law, I release Woof Riders, its owners, employees and agents from claims arising from my dog's participation, except those caused by gross negligence or willful misconduct, and I agree to indemnify them against claims caused by my dog.

8. Pickup and payment

I will pay for services as booked and pick up my dog at the agreed time. Late pickups after 30 minutes may be charged $1 per minute.

Initial each key section
